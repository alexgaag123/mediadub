1. INT/EXT. LAUNCH PAD. DAY.
As the title credits role, a distorted communication can be
heard as those in the control centre talk to those in the
rocket. The distortion clears and a voice is now heard
giving the final part of the count down for the rocket over
a loudspeaker:
                    CONTROL CENTER:
          We have main engine start: 4, 3, 2,
          1.

The scene cuts to the exterior of the launch pad as the
rocket is seen leaving the ground and beginning flight. The
dialogue from the next scene leaks into this.


2. EXT. STREET. DAY.
CELIA and THOM stand on a bridge in the middle of a quiet
town street. Celia grips the railings of the bridge with her
hands: one normal and one robotic. She has an attitude that
comes with annoyance, whilst Thom is cocky and confident.

                    CELIA:
          You’re a jerk, Thom.
                    THOM:
          Look, Celia, we have to follow our
          passions; you have your robotics
          and I just want to be awesome in
          space!
Celia turns to face Thom, her robot hand bunched into a
fist. Thom recoils from her, intimidated.
                    CELIA:
          Why don’t you just admit that
          you’re freaked out by my robot
          hand?!

                    THOM:
          I-I’m not freaked out by... It’s...
               (pauses)
          Alright, fine! I’m freaked out! I’m
          having nightmares that I’m being
          chased by these giant robotic claws
          of death!
                    CELIA:
               (Interrupts)
          Oh, whatever Thom! We’re done!

3. INT. CHURCH/BUNKER. NIGHT.
The argument from the last scene bleeds through to this one,
the actions of Thom and Celia being replicated by two
holographic figures. The figures distort and pause as a
computer controls them like a film, an ENGINEER pressing the
buttons.
                    ENGINEER:
          Robot’s memory synced and locked.

Cut to a close up of a brain in a container, respiration
machines can be heard breathing in the background as the
DIRECTOR, a large man with a robotic eye, stares at the
organ on display.
                    DIRECTOR:
          This is pretty freaky...

4. EXT/INT. CHURCH TOWER. NIGHT.
A hovering ship passes by with a quiet rumble as BARLEY sits
in wait, looking through his gun’s scope. An AGED THOM
sleeps on the floor nearby, a newspaper depicting the
creation of "killer robots" covering his face.
Barley focuses on the hovering ship and, with a switch
positioned on his gun, activates spotlights and music on the
previously quiet ship, waking up the aged Thom and drawing
robots to it, which begin to tear the ship apart until it
crashes.
                    BARLEY:
          Shouldn’t you be down there?

Thom sits upright and rubs his shoulder in discomfort.
                    AGED THOM:
          I heard you guys talking last
          night. It’s not my fault, you know?
Barley looks back at Thom briefly before returning to his
gun’s scope. Thom grabs the rope nearby and lowers himself
down the tower, as Barley sips his drink.

lands, and once again massages his shoulder from the strain.

24. INT. CHURCH/BUNKER. MORNING.

Several men in white overalls equip futuristic pulse guns to
their hands and walk to a position of cover. An alarm sounds
and robots break in through the glass windows above.
They start to fire the pulse guns as the robots bound
towards them, the occasional one shutting down from being
hit. One finally gets close enough to smash the area where
the men take cover.

25. INT. CHURCH TOWER. MORNING.
Barley gets up from the floor of the tower and approaches
the decent rope. He activates a belt of grenades and they
fall through the hole before him, bleeping on the way down.
He follows them shortly after.


26. INT. CHURCH/BUNKER. MORNING.
Barley reaches the bunker, and fires his gun at the robots
that surround him.
                    BARLEY:
          Urgh! COME ON!
After killing several of the robots, he pulls out another
grenade and throws it towards the large church organ on the
wall. It explodes and sends debris flying and knocks several
robots back. Barley continues to fire his gun at the
remaining targets.
                    BARLEY:
          RRRAAAHHHHHHH!!!!
Cut to the engineer and his computer’s holographic screen
showing a loading bar.
                    ENGINEER:
          Memory overwrite at 90 percent!
The Director sits in his chair, still watching the scene.
                    ASSISTANT:
          Captain! We have to abort!

The Director’s response is to hold his hands up in a "wait
just a moment" mannerism.


27. EXT. STREET. DAY
The robot, still holding Thom in its gigantic claw, lets him

28. INT. CHURCH/BUNKER. MORNING.
A robot attacks one of the generators in the bunker, causing
the holograms to give way. The younger Thom returns to his
true age, and the bridge returns to its decayed form.

                    AGED THOM:
          ...maybe we can too.
The Engineer watches as the progress bar reaches 100%.

                    ENGINEER:
          Memory overwrite complete!
He reaches down and pulls out one of the white pulse guns,
running off to join the fight.

Thom walks towards the robot and embraces it cautiously but
lovingly.
The Director sits back in his chair, still keeping his eye
on Thom and the robot.

                    DIRECTOR:
          There’s a lesson to be learned from
          this.
                    ASSISTANT:
          Could’a gone worse.

A robot approaches the two from behind, but the Assistant
stabs it in the eye with a nearby sword.
The final shot zooms out from Thom and the robot, who now
returns the embrace. Robots approach from all directions,
trapping him in.

29. EXT. STREET. DAY.

A mountain of robotic parts sits in the street, with the
